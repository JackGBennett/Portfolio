Why trust us?

The Intouch Foundation is a UK-registered charity committed to supporting people experiencing homelessness and severe disadvantage through practical, hands-on help such as, hot meals, and ongoing community support. As a registered charity, it is regulated by the Charity Commission for England and Wales, which means we must meet strict standards for governance, safeguarding, and financial transparency.

We publish our accounts and reports openly so you can see how funds are raised and how they are spent. This ensures accountability and shows that a large proportion of donations go directly toward frontline services supporting people in need. Our work is built around trust, transparency, and consistent community impact, so supporters can feel confident that their contributions are being used responsibly and effectively
