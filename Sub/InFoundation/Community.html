Our Community

Project Ayaana - previously known as InTouch Community is a Social welfare project being set up to promote community cohesion and work with those individuals who need our help the most locally and internationally. Project Ayaana works with communities and other charities to inspire them to make a positive difference in their local community or internally. We have many exciting projects

Our Work

Project Ayaana was set up in the memory of our Late Granddad Dr Mohammad Suliman who passed away in October 2014. Dr Suliman was a true humanitarian working as a field doctor in rural pakistan for over 50 years. His compassion and love to the most vulnerable inspired us to carry his legacy on.

#BringASmile

We are always looking and coming up with unique creative projects on how to bring a smile on someone face to break down barriers.

#SaveALife

Unique projects throughout the year to help support those in need locally and internationally.

#BeAHero

Unique projects which allows people to take part in exciting adrenaline filled activities while making a difference in their local community.

Our Vision

At Project Ayaana we believe that everyone wants to leave their mark on this world but not everyone knows how too. Everyone can leave their legacy by doing the smallest act of kindness and in the process of they can help change the world into a world we all want to live in. Our vision is to create a world where everyone respects and loves all.

Our core values are based on how our Dr Suliman lived his life and our Islamic Belief inspire us to keep working tirelessly to come up with unique innovative ways on how to engage the local communities to help those who are less fortunate – regardless of race, political affiliation, gender or belief locally and internationally.

We believe in empowering people to make a change.Our unique and integrated approach to inspiring the souls is transforming communities worldwide.

Our Mission

“Inspire the soul and watch the magic happen”

Our Mission is simply to inspire each soul we come across to leave their legacy! We work tirelessly to bring communities together to promote community cohesion and an understanding of all cultures and religions through the use of social action projects locally and internally to help bring an end to the suffering of others..
