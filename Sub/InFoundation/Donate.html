Donate

Your donation will enable our work to continue, together we can find new ways to prevent homelessness and feed the hungry.

A simple donation of £2.50 provides a warm, nutritious meal for someone in need within our community.

DONATING ONLINE

Make an online donation by credit or debit card through PayPal - it's quick, easy and secure. Please use the donate (for a single payment) and subscribe (for a regular monthly payment) buttons. If you want your donation to go towards a specific project (e.g. Feeding the homeless, donating to the foodbank, etc), please mention this in the comments when making your payment.
